## About section (page body)

Hi, we're Zyra Labs 👋

We're a small, community-minded software studio with a simple belief: the
best tools are the ones that respect you. No accounts you didn't ask for.
No ads, no telemetry, no "pro tier" holding the good features hostage.
Just software that does its job brilliantly and gets out of your way.

Some of what we build is commercial work — that's what keeps the lights
on. But some of it we build because *we* wanted it to exist, and those we
give to the community. **warpseed** is one of those: a free, blazing-fast
transfer client for Windows that pulls files off your seedbox at the full
speed of your line. Parallel connections, byte-level resume that survives
errors and restarts, and a queue you can actually trust with a 50 GB
transfer overnight. Paying monthly to move your own files never sat right
with us — so warpseed is free, and it stays free.

Free to download isn't free to make, though: real releases mean real
hours — testing against cranky servers, chasing the one bug that only
happens at 3 a.m., and shipping fixes when they're needed.

That's where the coffee comes in. ☕

If warpseed has saved you an evening of babysitting downloads, a coffee
says "keep going" louder than a thousand downloads ever could. It buys
development time on the community side of the ledger and tells us which
tools people actually care about.

No pressure, ever — warpseed works exactly the same whether you donate or
not. But if you'd like to fuel the next free release, we'd be genuinely
grateful.

**One more thing:** Zyra Labs is growing. If you're a builder — a
vibecoder, a tinkerer, or someone sitting on an app or business idea that
deserves to ship — we want to hear from you. Come find us at
zyralabs.tech.

Thanks for being here. Now go move some files — fast. 🚀

— Zyra Labs
